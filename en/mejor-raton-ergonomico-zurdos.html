How to choose

01Measure your hand before anything else

From the wrist crease to the tip of the middle finger: under 17.5 cm is size S, 17.5 to 19 cm is M and over 19 cm is L. A vertical mouse in the wrong size is worse than the flat mouse you already have.

02Vertical, ergonomic or trackball

A vertical mouse removes forearm rotation and asks for a week of adjustment. A classic ergonomic mouse works from the first minute. A trackball keeps the arm still and is what rests the shoulder most.

03The angle, if it is vertical

The closer to 60°, the more it corrects your posture and the more it depends on getting the size right. Between 55° and 57° the adjustment is gentler and the benefit is still clear.

04Connection and buttons

A dedicated 2.4 GHz receiver responds better; Bluetooth lets you hop between computers. Forward and back buttons save wrist movements all day long.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use a right-handed mouse with my left hand?

You can, but you lose the ergonomic advantage: the sculpted shape supports your palm the wrong way round and the side buttons end up under the wrong fingers. With a vertical, the tilt runs in the opposite direction to the one you need.

Is switching hands a good idea?

Switching hands spreads the load and is a recommended strategy once discomfort has set in. For that, an ambidextrous trackball is the most convenient option because it doesn't force you to buy a second device.
