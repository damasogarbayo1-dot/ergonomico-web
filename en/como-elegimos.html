How we choose

All our scores come from formulas applied equally to every product. Here they are, in full. If a score doesn't add up for you, you can recalculate it yourself.

Manufacturer specs only

Seat range, lumbar, sizes, warranty. No inflated stars, no fabricated reviews.

Published method

Every score comes from a formula you can read below, axis by axis.

Personalized to you

With your measurements, the ranking stops being generic: it's your fit %, product by product.

Transparent affiliation

If you buy through our links we earn a commission. We say so on every page.

What data we use

Only manufacturer-published specifications: seat height range, lumbar support type, armrests, maximum weight, EN 1335 certification, warranty, hand sizes, vertical angle, connectivity. We don't use store ratings or reviews we can't verify. When a spec isn't available we show “—”, we never make it up.

The formulas (0-10, over the catalog range)

Chairs

  • Adjustability: adjustable depth (2 pts) + lumbar (fixed 0.5 · adjustable 2 · active 3) + armrests (2D 1.5 · 3D 2 · 4D 2.5 · 5D 3) + width of the seat range (up to 2).
  • Lumbar support: fixed 3 · adjustable 7 · active 10.
  • Sturdiness: maximum weight normalized across the catalog (70 %) + EN 1335 certification (30 %).
  • Warranty and certification: warranty years out of 12 (80 %) + EN 1335 (20 %).
  • Value for money: average of the above divided by price, normalized across the catalog.

Mice and keyboards

  • Wrist neutrality: vertical ≥60° 10 · vertical 57° 9 · full split 10 · split 8.5 · curved 8 · wave 7 · trackball 7 · classic ergonomic 5.5.
  • Versatility: wireless (4) + left-handed friendly (3) + number of sizes covered (1 per size).
  • Size coverage: 1 size 3 · 2 sizes 6 · 3 sizes 10.
  • Value for money: same as for chairs.

Scores are recalculated with every catalog update (if a better product comes in, the relative scores of the others can drop). They are a calculated editorial rating, not a manufacturer figure.

Your fit %

It's our star metric and it's personal: it crosses your measurements (height → popliteal height ≈ 25 %, weight, hours per day, hand size, dominant hand) with each product's specs. It's calculated in your browser and your measurements never leave your device.

How we make money

Through affiliate links (brand programs and Amazon): if you buy through them we earn a commission at no extra cost to you. The commission does not alter the scores — the formula is the same for products with and without an affiliate program. Affiliate disclosure.
