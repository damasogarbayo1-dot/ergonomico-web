Quick ergonomics tips

What fixes 80% of workstations, without buying anything.

01The 25% rule

Your ideal seat height ≈ height × 0.25. It is the first filter for any chair and rules out more models than any other spec.

02Screen at eye level

The top edge of the monitor at eye level, an arm's length away. If you use a laptop, you need a stand and a separate keyboard.

03Elbows at 90°

If the desk does not go down, raise the chair and add a footrest. If the desk goes up, even better: alternate sitting and standing.

04Breaks every 50 minutes

No chair replaces moving. Not even the Aeron. Stand up for two minutes and look into the distance to rest your eyes.

05Measure your hand before buying a mouse

From the wrist crease to the tip of the middle finger: under 17.5 cm is S, 17.5 to 19 is M, over 19 is L.
