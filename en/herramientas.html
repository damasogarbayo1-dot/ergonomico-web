How do they connect?

They are not two loose toys: they are a system that starts with your measurements and ends in a decision.

01You measure once

ErgoCheck calculates your popliteal, desk and screen heights. It is stored in your browser, not on a server of ours.

02Everything gets personalized

Each category's filters are pre-filled with those measurements and the catalogue shows your fit % product by product.

03You decide with evidence

The comparator pits your finalists against each other and gives a verdict calculated from your measurements, not from opinions.

Already have finalists?

Take them to the comparator and see who wins at what, with a spec-by-spec table and a verdict.
