What defines it

01

From 62 to 125 cm in height

It must cover your two working heights: sitting (≈ 43% of your height) and standing (≈ 62%). The typical flaw of cheap ones is falling short at the top.

02

Dual motor

Rises faster, quieter and with more load than a single motor. If you alternate posture several times a day, you notice it every time.

03

4 memory positions

Your exact heights at one button. When changing posture costs ten seconds of adjustment, you stop doing it: presets are what make the desk get used.
