Manufacturer specs · reviewed on 2026-09-15

Mechanical keyboard with a 65% Alice layout, CNC-machined aluminium body of 358 × 136 mm and 1,820 g, gasket mount and a programmable knob. Hot-swappable Gateron G Pro switches (3 and 5 pin), south-facing RGB, 1,000 Hz polling and QMK/VIA programming; it connects via USB-C cable. Spanish ISO layout available from Keychron's European store.
