How to choose

01Your two working heights

Sitting you need around 43% of your height and standing, around 62%. A standing desk only works if its travel covers both: the expensive mistake is falling short at the top.

02Count the thickness of the tabletop

Frames state their height without a tabletop. If you buy the structure separately, add 1.8 to 2.5 cm: that difference decides whether you reach your standing height or not.

03Motor, load and noise

Dual motor for two screens or a heavy load: it rises faster, wobbles less as you type and makes less noise. A single motor is enough for a light tabletop with one monitor.

04Presets and safety

Without height presets, most people stop changing posture within two weeks. Anti-collision saves you from crushing a drawer unit, and the child lock saves you from surprises with kids around.

Frequently asked questions

What standing height do I need if I am 1.90 m tall?

Around 118 cm (62% of your height, at elbow level). A desk that stops at 120 cm is just enough; below 118, it is not.

What if the desk does not rise enough?

There is no comfortable fix: raising the top with risers compromises stability. It is better to pay for the right range from the start.
