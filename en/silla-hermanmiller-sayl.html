What defines it

01

Seat from 41.9 to 55.9 cm

It is the first filter for any chair: your popliteal height (≈ 25% of your height) must fall within this range. The chair filter checks it against your measurements in one click.

02

Fixed lumbar support

The backrest has a built-in lumbar curve, with no adjustment. It works if your back matches it; on long days it usually falls short.

03

12-year warranty · up to 159 kg

The declared maximum weight is the frame's test limit, and the warranty is the best clue to its real durability.
